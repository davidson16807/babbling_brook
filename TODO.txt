* "Liquid" archetype component:
	top_texture1
	top_texture2
	freezing_temperature
	frozen_texture
	viscosity
	unpassable
	when frozen, frozen_texture displays on all sides and tile acts as a solid
	when melted, tile texture displays is animated by uv tricks
	when melted, and when temperature is greater than max_safe_temperature passed to a constructor for the class that stores tiles on application startup, tile is unpassable
	animations speed is linear to viscosity attribute and greatest diagonal height difference of tile
	ground for liquid tiles is calculated as an offset to ground for solid tiles, calcualted from a wave_depth, wave_depth_amplitude, wave_period, and wave_phase, so that characters appear to bob and float in it
	wave_phase is stored in phase table, other wave_* variables are passed to the constructor to the class that calculates ground height

* tweak inventory table so that it is represented as (character, item, quantity) tuples and allows representing inventories of arbitrary characters

* dynamic character generation
	* edited svgs are created from template and stored for each character upon application startup along with entries in character animation table
	* individual templates for toddlers, children, and adult body morphs
	* middle-age adults have bags over eyes
	* old people have liver warts on one temple of their head
	* standing, walking, and running animations accomplished by shifting upper body layers along vertical axis
	* blinking accomplished by horizontal eye rectangles
	* anger accomplished by diagonal eye rectangles, lowered inward
	* sadness accomplished by diagonal eye rectangles, raised inward
	* happiness accomplished by pink blush on cheeks and diagonal eye rectangles, raised inward
	* hurt status accomplished horizontal "sad" eye rectangles and rotating entire svg by 90 degrees
	* scaling for fat and short people
	* 4 skin colors, represented by id that maps to color according to id→color lookup passed at construction
	* 4 genetic hair colors, represented by id that maps to color according to id→color lookup passed at construction
	* old people are either bald or grey depending on sex and gene
	* shirt color and pants color
	* women wear long dresses
	* soldiers wear armor, shield, and spear
	* wealthy wear brighter vibrant colors, belt with gold buckle, and shirt with trim
	* poor wear darker muted colors, and a patch on one pant leg
