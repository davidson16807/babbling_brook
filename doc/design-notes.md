# Babbling Brook design notes

Off-screen State of play is communicated strictly through scouts within the party
	Fill-in-blank questions
	Yes/no questions?
	Comprehension 
	Positional declensions
	Present, progressive
Inventory for each party member is controlled by command
	Possessives
	Possession, transfer
	Accusative, dative
Motion of party members is controlled by command
	Going, walking, running
	Positional declensions
	Imperative, jussive, future
	Formality
	Formation
State of party members world model is controlled by statements made to them
	Formation
	Conjugation
	Positional declensions
	Adjectives
